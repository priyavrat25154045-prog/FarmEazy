Market Challenges

The Ground Reality: Critical Farming Bottlenecks

Over 86% of Indian farmers are smallholders trapped between high capital machinery costs and seasonal labor shortages.

💸

Prohibitive Capital Cost

Modern tractors and harvesters cost ₹6.5 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs. Smallholder farmers cannot buy heavy equipment that remains unutilized for 8 to 9 months every year.

⏱️

Peak Seasonal Shortages

During the critical 7-10 day sowing and harvesting windows, local village equipment gets booked out, causing crop spoilage, exorbitant rates, and huge yield losses.

📉

Equipment Underutilization

Over 40% of India's 1.2M+ tractors sit idle outside peak cycles. Owners lack a digital platform to find nearby farmers who need tillage, spraying, or harvesting.

System Architecture

Full-Stack System Architecture

A resilient, responsive, and secure tech stack designed for fast loading even on rural 4G connections.

🖥️ FRONTEND Vanilla HTML5, Glassmorphism CSS design system, Responsive layout (desktop to smartphone), Vanilla JavaScript single-page controllers.
⚡ BACKEND SERVER Node.js with Express.js REST API, Helmet security hardening, express-session cookie authentication, and rate limiting.
🗄️ PERSISTENT DATABASE Relational SQLite (SQL.js) with isolated storage (database/farmeazy.sqlite), bcrypt credential hashing, and foreign key role separation.
